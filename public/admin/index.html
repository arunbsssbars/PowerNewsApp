PowerNews Architectural Playbook & System Manual

          

            This playbook provides the complete technical manual for our ecosystem: the full ingestion-to-mobile pipeline mechanics, the Power60 algorithm mathematics, how to use our collected Cloud Firestore dataset to fine-tune self-hosted models for zero API cost, known system flaws, and the strategic roadmap.
          

        


        

          
          
          

            

              1
              

                
End-to-End Pipeline Architecture (How the Current System Works)

                
The 6-stage lifecycle from raw publisher web pages to offline Flutter rendering.

              

            


            

              
              

                

                   1.1 Web Scraping & RSS Crawling
                

                

                  Runs every 20 minutes via node-cron in server/index.js. Queries 15+ verified energy sources including PIB Ministry of Power, Mercom India, PV Magazine, Financial Express, Economic Times Energy, LiveMint, and CERC orders. Uses axios with rotating User-Agent headers and cheerio HTML extraction.
                

              


              

                

                   1.2 Data Cleaning & Deduplication
                

                

                  contentCleaner.js strips promotional banners, tracking scripts, HTML tags, and ads. Each article computes a unique SHA-256 fingerprint from title + source. Duplicate articles within the 7-day retention window are collapsed into a single multi-source link record.
                

              


              

                

                   1.3 Non-Power Discarding (PowerFilter)
                

                

                  rules.js executes positive energy taxonomy matching (generation, transmission, distribution, tariffs, DISCOMs, SCADA) and negative exclusion filters (general politics, celebrity news, cricket, lifestyle). Any article failing the threshold is discarded before invoking Gemini AI.
                

              


              

                

                   1.4 AI Summarization (60 Words)
                

                

                  Google Gemini 2.5 Flash receives the sanitized full text. The system prompt strictly enforces: "Write a factual, concise 60-word narrative briefing for power engineers. Focus on MW capacity, investment (Rs. Cr), tariffs, and discom impact. Do not use bullet points or fluff."
                

              


              

                

                   1.5 Cloud Firestore Storage
                

                

                  firestoreService.js writes 14 structured fields to the ai_summaries collection. Gatekeeper enforces summaries ≥ 75 chars and non-bullet narrative. A daily 03:30 AM cron purges records older than 30 days if storage approaches the 850 MB high-watermark.
                

              


              

                

                   1.6 Dual-Layer Flutter Mobile Display
                

                

                  SQLite (powernews_offline.db): 0ms instant app launch, offline browsing, search history, and bookmarks.
                  
REST API Sync (/api/news): Pulls freshest vetted articles in background, hydrates SQLite, and applies gravity score sorting.

The Power60 Algorithm & Feed Ranking Formulation

                
Mathematical weighting equation preventing solar clumping and highlighting critical power verticals.

              

            


            

              

                

                  Score = (Base + (KW_Matches × 50) + SourceWeight - SolarPenalty) / (AgeInHours + 2)^1.5
                

                

                  
Base Score (100 pts): Initial baseline for every verified, AI-summarized power story.

                  
Keyword Multiplier (+50 pts / match): Automatically checks title and summary against all active keywords (e.g. STATCOM, BESS, Green Ammonia, Substation, SCADA). Each match adds +50 points.

                  
Source Authority Weight (+80 / -20 pts): Technical journals (e.g. PowerLine, Press Releases) receive +80; high-volume aggregator feeds receive -20 to prevent feed saturation.

                  
Category Penalty (-30 pts): Generic solar news penalized in the default feed to allow transmission, OEM technology, and DISCOM updates to surface.

                  
Time Decay Gravity: Divides by (AgeInHours + 2)^1.5 so breaking news stays on top while older stories gracefully move down.

                

              


              

                

                  🛡 Anti-Clumping Engine
                

                

                  In India, renewable and rooftop solar announcements generate 4x higher article volume than transmission, substation, and DISCOM news. Without intervention, solar would occupy 80% of the mobile feed.
                

                

                  The Rule: If 2 consecutive articles in the feed belong to the Solar/Renewables category, the ranking engine automatically searches forward and swaps the third solar article with the next available non-solar story (T&D, SCADA, Tariffs, or Generation).

Creating the Power60 Model: Step-by-Step Fine-Tuning Guide

                
How to use our 1,277+ Firestore training pairs to train a specialized model and achieve $0 API costs.

              

            


            

              

                
Step 1: Export .jsonl Training Dataset

                

                  Navigate to the ML Training tab and click "Download .jsonl Dataset". The endpoint /api/admin/export-dataset queries approved documents from power60_training_data and formats them into instruction pairs: {"messages": [{"role": "user", "content": originalText}, {"role": "model", "content": geminiSummary}]}.
                

              


              

                
Step 2: Fine-Tune with LoRA/QLoRA on Google Colab / Kaggle

                

                  Upload power60_dataset.jsonl to Google Colab (free T4 GPU). Use Unsloth with base model unsloth/llama-3-8b-Instruct-bnb-4bit or mistral-7b-instruct-v0.3. Set training parameters: 3 epochs, learning rate 2e-4, batch size 2, gradient accumulation 4, LoRA rank 16. The model learns our exact Indian power sector vocabulary and 60-word concise structure.
                

              


              

                
Step 3: Quantize to GGUF & Deploy for Zero Ongoing Cost

                

                  Export fine-tuned LoRA weights merged with the base model into GGUF 4-bit format (power60-8b-Q4_K_M.gguf). Deploy on a small VPS or container running Ollama or vLLM. Update geminiService.js to point to the local inference URL, eliminating all recurring Gemini API costs and bypassing external rate limits forever.

              5
              

                
Current Flaws & The Way Ahead (Architectural Roadmap)

                
Critical analysis of system bottlenecks and the engineering plan to solve them.

              

            


            

              

                

                  
                  Current Bottlenecks & Flaws
                

                
    
                  
  • Render Free-Tier Sleep: The backend process sleeps after 15 mins of idle time, resulting in a 30-50s cold boot delay for the first morning app user.
    • 
                  
  • Gemini Rate Limits (15 RPM): Sudden spikes in breaking news ingestion can cause HTTP 429 quota exhaustion on Gemini free tier.
    • 
                  
  • Substring Keyword Matching: Exact string matching misses semantic synonyms (e.g. "BESS" vs "Battery Energy Storage System").
    • 
                  
  • Syndicate Wire Duplication: The same PTI/ANI wire story printed across 4 newspapers can create multiple cards if headlines vary slightly.
    • 
                

              


              

                

                  
                  The Way Ahead (Engineering Roadmap)
                

                
    
                  
  • Semantic Deduplication via Vectors: Compute vector embeddings on headlines and cluster stories with cosine similarity > 0.88 under a single card with multi-publisher links.
    • 
                  
  • Self-Hosted Power60 LLM: Fine-tune and deploy our own 8B open model on a dedicated container to achieve 100% independence from external APIs.
    • 
                  
  • Decoupled Worker Queue (BullMQ / Redis): Offload web scraping and AI inference into background queue workers so HTTP requests remain instantaneous.
    • 
                  
  • FCM Push Notifications: Automatically push breaking grid alerts and CERC regulatory orders to user mobile notifications.
    •

Operational Guide & Purpose of Every Portal Tab

                
Quick reference explaining the role and controls of each screen.

              

            


            

              

                
📱 Flutter Feed Tab

                

                  Displays the exact vetted pool of articles delivered to the mobile app. Shows live PowerScore, word count, and confirms that only genuine AI summaries within the 7-day window are shown.
                

              

              

                
⚡ Keywords Tab

                

                  Manages active energy entities that trigger a +50 PowerScore lift. Includes automatic duplicate rejection and auto-discovery powered by the 04:00 AM & PM IST cron.
                

              

              

                
🤖 ML Training Tab

                

                  Enables human editorial review of scraped text vs Gemini summaries. Allows single-click Approve/Reject and one-click download of the `.jsonl` fine-tuning dataset.
                

              

              

                
🩺 Health Tab

                

                  Real-time telemetry of process uptime, RSS/Heap memory usage, and Firestore document counts. Guaranteed zero layout shift with non-intrusive backdrop overlays.
